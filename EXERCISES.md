# Module 3A — Project Challenges

These challenges are deliberately designed so that you have to **work things out**.

You will be taught the relevant concepts, but the project will not give you model answers.
Some exercises have several reasonable answers. Your reasoning and evidence matter.

## Rules

- Use `requirements/requirements.md` as your main test basis.
- Do not inspect `public/app.js` to hunt for defects unless the instructor explicitly asks you to.
- Do not invent missing business rules. Record questions and assumptions.
- A failed test does not automatically prove that the product is defective.
- When asked to justify a decision, an answer without a reason is incomplete.
- Keep evidence of what you actually observed.
- Work only against the supplied local training application.

## Difficulty

**Core** — everyone should attempt it.  
**Investigation** — you decide the approach and defend it.  
**Stretch** — optional extension if you finish early.

## Three-day journey

**Day 1:** understand the product, analyse requirements, design and execute tests.  
**Day 2:** automate selected behaviour, troubleshoot, and respond to a product change.  
**Day 3:** express behaviour with BDD/Cucumber, consider security, and make a release recommendation.
